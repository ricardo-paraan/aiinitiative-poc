## 🎉 What Will Happen Now:

The `ERR_INCOMPLETE_CHUNKED_ENCODING` error was caused by:

- The Ticket entity has `@OneToMany` relationships with attachments and comments
- When serializing to JSON, it tried to include those relationships
- Those relationships pointed back to Ticket (circular reference)
- This caused an infinite loop and crashed the serialization

__The fix:__

- Added `@JsonIgnoreProperties({"attachments", "comments"})`
- This tells Jackson to ignore those fields when converting to JSON
- Your tickets will now serialize cleanly without the circular reference
